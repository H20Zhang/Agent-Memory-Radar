## Reader-facing windows

The repository landing page deliberately becomes coarser with time:

- **Weekly — last ~31 days:** preserve local changes, disagreements, and reading order.
- **Monthly — latest 3 calendar months:** compress weekly movement into stronger/weakening themes and falsification conditions.
- **Yearly — all available years:** retain only the most durable shifts and corrections.

Older weekly/monthly compactions remain in their archive directories after aging out of the homepage.

## Compaction standard

Weekly, monthly, and yearly reports are **not concatenated paper summaries**. They should identify design-space movement, compare evidence, preserve contradictions, and state what future evidence would change the current interpretation.

Raw daily provenance lives under `runs/daily/YYYY/MM/DD.md` and is not the primary reading interface.
