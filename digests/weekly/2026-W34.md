## The week in one sentence

The week's new evidence did not establish a new memory architecture. It sharpened the **decision boundaries that must be qualified before persistent state affects a later consumer**: evidence completion, sparse skill exposure, judge competence, harness commit, and training-side experience reuse.

## What actually changed

### 1. Multi-source memory needs an evidence-completion control

**MemFuse** preserves source provenance while joining fragmented support, but its largest ablation belongs to the answer-time retrieval loop rather than graph construction or fusion. Overall scores reach **0.4659 / 0.4574 / 0.4698**, while removing agentic retrieval costs **0.1036**—far more than removing graph/fusion overall.

**So what:** a fused store should not receive causal credit for an access loop's work. The closest next control is a provenance-preserving raw or minimally transformed archive with the same iterative search policy, final evidence budget, and complete ingestion-plus-serving cost.

### 2. Procedural-memory updates need separate selector, judge, and commit gates

Three papers expose different gates and must not be collapsed into one trend.

- **SkillGate** adds an oracle-local selector channel before fixed skill files are exposed. Success rises **47.0%→53.2%**, oracle exposure **54.3%→83.9%**, and misleading exposure falls **69.6%→21.8%**. The intervention couples privileged utility labels, read-call masking, and selector-mass normalization, so the gain is not attributable to one credit mechanism.
- **Competence, Not Accuracy** asks whether a reference-free judge is discriminative on the paired candidate distribution it will govern. Factual-QA AUC falls **0.855→0.735** after item-difficulty control; research-math within-question AUC is **0.489**, near chance.
- **Harness Continual Learning** makes the entire non-parametric harness—not memory alone—the commit unit. A historical-loss bound improves the final aggregate from **60.13** unrestricted to **63.46** at `b=1`; the narrower memory-update ablation is **62.28 / 0.83 forgetting** versus **63.41 / 0.45** for Full HCL.

**So what:** selecting a persistent procedure, judging its candidate update, and committing a co-adaptive harness are three different causal stages. A system-level score cannot assign the package gain to memory.

### 3. Persistent state can act before deployment, in experience generation

**SPADE** stores executable environments and regret metadata across training episodes, then retrieves a few skill-matched examples for the next Environment Designer prompt. The selected full checkpoint averages **58.3** across eight game benchmarks versus **53.2** for the official no-memory launcher, while AIME 2026 moves **75.0→74.4** with memory.

**So what:** training-side memory deserves separate accounting from deployed actor memory. The current result is one package ablation: single runs, suite-selected checkpoints, unmatched demonstration tokens, and unreported compute prevent attribution to regret ranking, the 200-record buffer, or FIFO retention.

## Tensions worth tracking

- **Organization vs access:** MemFuse makes fused provenance visible, but answer-time evidence completion carries the largest measured effect.
- **Local supervision vs open libraries:** SkillGate's verified single-oracle setting does not yet cover evolving, compositional, or conflicting skill pools.
- **Gate interpretability vs usable discrimination:** a judge can look plausible at the aggregate level while failing the same-question comparison required for admission.
- **Memory component vs state package:** HCL and SPADE both contain persistent memory, but their headline outcomes arise from co-adaptive training systems.

## Research gaps exposed this week

The highest-value experiment is an end-to-end **persistent-state promotion protocol**. Hold the actor, candidate stream, and task fixed; independently vary access, selection, judge, commit, and rollback; then report paired downstream behavior, protected-slice regression, provenance, and full ingestion/access/maintenance cost.

A second gap is replication across seeds and naturally evolving workloads. All five directions rely on one paper and one narrow intervention family; none earns a durable Field Map change.

## Evidence to watch next

The week's interpretation strengthens if provenance-preserving fusion beats a matched iterative raw-state controller; selector-local supervision transfers beyond a known oracle skill; judge qualification predicts safer real commits; guarded harness updates survive longer streams; and training-side memory beats token-matched static, random, and score-shuffled replay.

It weakens if these differences disappear once prompt length, selection budget, judge distribution, seed variance, and complete lifecycle cost are matched.
