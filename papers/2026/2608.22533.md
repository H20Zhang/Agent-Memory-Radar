# CONTRAMEM: Learning Self-Evolving Procedural Memory from Contrasting Multi-Model Trajectories

[← Latest Papers](../../README.en.md#latest-papers) · [Research Map](../../README.en.md#field-map) · [Memory Learning & Evolution](../../categories/memory-learning-evolution.md) · [中文](2608.22533.zh.md)

**Paper:** [arXiv:2608.22533](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22533) · **Published:** 2026-08-23 · **Importance:** 4/5 · **Confidence:** Medium

> **Research delta.** Same-task outcome variation can supervise a compact procedural bank. The evidence supports heterogeneous contrast plus curation, not online self-evolution or contrast alone.

## Problem

One successful trajectory omits failures, recovery, and cross-model distinctions needed for transferable procedures.

## Mechanism

CONTRAMEM normalizes trajectories, reflects on outcome differences, emits localized deltas, and curates Function and Skill Cards through ADD/PATCH/MERGE/NARROW/NOOP. At test time BM25 supplies at most three cards before tool use.

## Compared with

The closest controls are no memory and self-memory in the same pipeline, append-only with the same deltas/retrieval, and three matched GPT-5.5 rollouts versus three heterogeneous sources.

## Decisive evidence

Held-out source-model macro success is **26.2%** no-memory, **47.2%** self-memory, and **55.3%** shared contrastive memory; unseen Qwen moves **18.5→35.5**. Under matched three-trajectory construction, **70.0→77.5**. Curation raises append-only **65.0→71.9** while shrinking **273→205 cards**.

## Main caveat

The bank is frozen during evaluation, so this is offline construction. Broader successful-behavior coverage is an alternative attribution; 23 regressions include Claude Adaptability and Time. Source generation, reflection, verifier/judge, maintenance, latency, dollars, and energy are incomplete.

## Memory lifecycle

| Stage | Design |
|---|---|
| Write | Heterogeneous trajectories are contrasted into procedural deltas. |
| Organize | Local curation maintains Function and Skill Card banks. |
| Read | BM25 exposes at most three cards as pre-tool guidance. |
| Update / forget | Offline merge/narrow is tested; online update and stale-card retirement are not. |

## Why it matters

Procedural memory may learn more from controlled outcome variation than from accumulating successes, but coverage and contrast must be separated.
