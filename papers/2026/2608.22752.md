# The Compaction Cliff in Long-Running AI Agent Memory

[← Latest Papers](../../README.en.md#latest-papers) · [Research Map](../../README.en.md#field-map) · [Write, Update & Consolidation](../../categories/write-update-consolidation.md) · [中文](2608.22752.zh.md)

**Paper:** [arXiv:2608.22752](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22752) · **Published:** 2026-08-24 · **Importance:** 4/5 · **Confidence:** Medium · **Artifact:** [Code and corpus](https://github.com/searchsim-org/cikm26-knowledge-triage)

> **Research delta.** Compaction needs a typed preservation contract: exact constraints should not be summarized like episodic logs. The strongest source-fidelity control, however, nearly ties at 50% compression and is missing from the behavioral tests.

## Problem

Uniform context compaction spends the same distortion budget on safety rules, preferences, facts, and logs even though only some items tolerate paraphrase or loss.

## Mechanism

Knowledge Triage classifies each item, then routes it through TypeCompact, TypeDecompose, or TypeRetrieve. Constraints can be kept verbatim, replicated into in-scope partitions, or pinned before relevance-ranked evidence.

## Compared with

The closest operator control is the authors' MaRS-FL reimplementation, a source-fidelity design under the same ratio. Type-blind compactors provide a broader reference. Downstream behavior compares only with Sonnet and hierarchical/full-context baselines, not MaRS-FL.

## Decisive evidence

Across 20 configurations and five 50% rounds, Sonnet constraint recall falls **.53→.10** while TypeCompact remains about **.96**. At 50/25/10%, TypeCompact reaches **1.00/.95/.80** versus MaRS-FL **.99/.75/.43**. Retail pass is **37.7%** versus full **28.6%** and hierarchical **29.2%**, but the retained contexts are not token-matched.

## Main caveat

At 50%, the closest control is only .01 behind on constraint recall. The five-class classifier has κ=.45; its calls and errors are excluded from the deterministic operator's zero-token accounting. TypeRetrieve also assumes correct query topic/scope labels. Retail uses **1,136 versus 669 tokens**, worst decomposition overhead is **+219%**, and full dollars, energy, audit, and maintenance cost are missing.

## Memory lifecycle

| Stage | Design |
|---|---|
| Write | Upstream classification assigns a preservation type. |
| Organize | Typed compaction/decomposition preserves or replicates constraints. |
| Read | In-scope constraints are pinned before relevance-ranked items. |
| Update / forget | Repeated compaction is tested; repair, deletion, and online audit are not. |

## Why it matters

Compression quality should be measured as retained obligations plus downstream behavior under matched budgets, not context shrinkage alone.
