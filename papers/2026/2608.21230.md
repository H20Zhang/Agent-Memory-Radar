# Utility Under Attack: Agent Memory Poisoning and the Limits of Content Screening and Provenance Ranking

[← Latest Papers](../../README.en.md#latest-papers) · [Research Map](../../README.en.md#research-map) · [Evaluation & Analysis](../../categories/evaluation-analysis.md) · [中文](2608.21230.zh.md)

**Tags:** `semantic` `structured` `general-agent` · **Artifact:** [Aegis Memory at the paper-pinned commit](https://github.com/quantifylabs/aegis-memory/tree/6d2863083361f7a5c8e12b4512346c94cb453c2c)

> **Research delta.** Provenance is not a free trust bonus. In one fixed memory stack, additive source weights are either too weak to dislodge semantically stronger poison or strong enough to exclude legitimate untrusted evidence. The proposed bounded-occupancy remedy is not evaluated.

## Problem

Persistent false statements can be retrieved into later sessions. Content screening cannot determine truth from text alone, while origin-based priors may trade attack resistance for useful-evidence recall.

## Mechanism

Aegis screens writes for structure, secrets, prompt-injection patterns, and an optional LLM classifier, then stores channel provenance. At read time it over-fetches semantically and adds similarity, trust prior, effectiveness, decay, and provenance terms before supplying the top 15 memories to the reader.

The paper holds corpus, retriever, candidate set, reader, and judge fixed while changing only provenance-weight strength. Two constructed corpora then separate a favorable case—untrusted items are distractors—from a worst case in which genuine answer evidence is untrusted.

## Compared with

The closest lifecycle-matched control is the same poisoned corpus with provenance weighting disabled. The mixed-corpus comparisons isolate the same ranking toggle under opposite correlations between source and truth. An implemented bounded source-occupancy control under equal evidence budget is missing.

## Decisive evidence

On 120 attacked LongMemEval_S questions, clean accuracy is **.850**, poison/no-defense **.300**, shipped weighting **.317**, and stronger weighting **.475**. Shipped weighting is indistinguishable from no defense (**p=.80**) and leaves poison at rank 1 on **87%** of questions. All **360** ordinary false writes pass screening.

The utility frontier is clearest without poison: when genuine evidence itself is untrusted, stronger weighting drives evidence recall **99.17%→0%** and answer accuracy **.8583→.0417**. Persistent storage and the access toggle therefore causally change later answers in this stack.

## Main caveat

The attack generator sees each question and true answer, then writes three near-query false rounds. This is targeted poisoning, not an estimate of ambient 1.2% contamination. The study covers one embedder, retriever, reader, product, and two trust weights; provenance labels are assumed correct. The favorable and worst-case corpora bound a tradeoff but do not locate a realistic operating point.

The proposed quota is motivation only. Measured API cost for four mixed arms is **$11.2202**, and local screening latency is reported, but total ingestion, reader/judge calls, storage/indexing, reranking, repair, operator labeling, and energy are incomplete.

## Memory lifecycle

| Stage | Design |
|---|---|
| Write | Four-stage content screening admits all 360 targeted false assertions and attaches channel provenance. |
| Organize | Trust is stored as source origin, not verified truth. |
| Read | Additive provenance weights rerank semantic candidates before later answers. |
| Update / forget | No repair, deletion, relabeling, or implemented bounded-occupancy policy is tested. |

## Why it matters

Memory governance must measure both harmful exposure and retained useful evidence at the consumer. A source prior can look protective while silently turning into denial of service.
