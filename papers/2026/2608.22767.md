# The Retriever Should Remember: Experience-Amortized Reranking for Long-Term Agent Memory

[← Latest Papers](../../README.en.md#latest-papers) · [Research Map](../../README.en.md#field-map) · [Retrieval & Access](../../categories/retrieval-access.md) · [中文](2608.22767.zh.md)

**Paper:** [arXiv:2608.22767](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22767) · **Published:** 2026-08-24 · **Importance:** 3/5 · **Confidence:** Low · **Artifact:** [Code](https://github.com/FengQi-HITSZ/earm)

> **Research delta.** A retriever can persist query–memory relevance experience and amortize later reranking. The clean completion increment is modest and fixed ordering may supply hidden structure.

## Problem

Stateless LLM reranking repeatedly scores a growing candidate pool and discards the relevance evidence after each query.

## Mechanism

EARM stores sparse direct scores in a query-by-memory matrix, completes missing entries with temporally masked low-rank ALS, and mixes observed and estimated scores to rerank a fixed semantic candidate set.

## Compared with

Observed-only uses the same directly scored anchors without completion. Semantic retrieval and full LLM reranking bound accuracy and call cost.

## Decisive evidence

Top-10 accuracy is **82.21** semantic, **88.83** observed-only, and **91.62** full EARM: completion adds **2.79 points**, not the full package's 9.41. Reranker calls fall **307,982→78,736**. Rank-2 temporal completion instead moves **81.31→80.06**.

## Main caveat

The content store and candidate set are fixed. Stable IDs and question order may create structure unavailable after shuffle, growth, edit, or delete. No seeds or uncertainty are reported; ALS/storage growth, latency, price, ingestion, maintenance, and energy are missing.

## Memory lifecycle

| Stage | Design |
|---|---|
| Write | Direct relevance judgments are stored as retrieval experience. |
| Organize | Temporally masked matrix completion estimates missing scores. |
| Read | Mixed scores rerank a fixed semantic candidate set. |
| Update / forget | Scores accumulate; drift, delete, and matrix retirement are not tested. |

## Why it matters

Access policy can be persistent learned state, but it must survive shuffled, growing, editable stores before becoming a durable direction.
