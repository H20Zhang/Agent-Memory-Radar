## Weekly compaction — 2026-W33

W33 (2026-08-10 through 2026-08-16) is closed and `digests/weekly/2026-W33.md` has been corrected from **15 to 16 canonical papers** to include the Aug 14 backfill.

The stage-factorized thesis remains intact but gets one important refinement:

1. **Access needs matched controls:** ReFind raises raw-record/stateful search as the baseline; RippleMem shows structure still earns complexity when it enables planned evidence completion under a more matched control; MESA and MAP-Graph add selective exposure and admissibility.
2. **Retrieval is not reuse:** QCR isolates target-conditioned rebinding; Demystifying Agent Skills shows the same source experience becomes more useful as a standardized procedural artifact and that exact ground-truth retrieval is not a sufficient utility metric; HyMeS supplies a complementary embodied case.
3. **Self-improvement splits into feedback, evolvable access, and governance:** SkillEvo isolates feedback-source quality, ERSkill evolves the read program/router, and Practice Makes Unsafe separates unsafe authoring, retrieval, and later harm.
4. **Lifecycle cost/forgetting require separate accounting:** LycheeMemory V2 separates batching from semantic boundaries, Total Recall meters end-to-end serving break-even, and Sleeping Agent shows field-specific information loss under gist compression.

The strongest methodological conclusion remains: **credit memory complexity only at the stage where a matched simpler alternative fails**.
