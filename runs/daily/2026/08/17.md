1. **Access needs matched controls:** ReFind raises raw-record/stateful search as the baseline; RippleMem shows structure still earns complexity when it enables planned evidence completion under a more matched control; MESA and MAP-Graph add selective exposure and admissibility.

The strongest methodological conclusion is: **credit memory complexity only at the stage where a matched simpler alternative fails**.
