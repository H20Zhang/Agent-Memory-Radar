## Skeptical-review corrections

**V-Mem:** the initial temptation is to summarize it as generated cross-modal query anchors. The ablation rejects that framing: vanilla retrieval scores 0.680, routing 0.689, shared-round matching 0.818, and generated anchors 0.825. The research note and README therefore treat **shared interaction structure** as the main contribution.

**RoMeRL:** the method reduces the Memory-Reward Trap by shrinking and semantically structuring the feedback-bearing state; it does **not** solve causal credit assignment. The note avoids claiming causal memory utility.

**SkillJack:** the lifecycle failure is important, but most attack-success measurements are routing-level policy-violation proxies rather than successful live compromise. The 20% clean-task trigger rate is surfaced rather than hidden.

**Muscle Memory:** the representation shift from retrieved procedure to executable specialist is interesting, but the comparison is not strong enough to attribute gains to compilation itself. It was therefore rated 3/5 rather than 4/5.
