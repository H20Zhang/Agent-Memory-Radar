## Skeptical-review corrections

**MESA:** the headline 65.1% is less informative than the structure-selection controls. Route-to-one is 57.0%; all five views reach 63.7% with 18.7k evidence tokens; MESA reaches 65.1% with 11.0k. However, a validation-selected domain-conditioned policy already reaches 64.4%, so the evidence strongly supports *selective composition* but only modestly supports fine-grained learned selection over a good coarse router.

**MAP-Graph:** aggregate utility is insufficient. Removing the permission filter can raise utility while conditional unauthorized access becomes 100%; the durable result is the separation of read eligibility, inherited trust, and action-risk sufficiency, not “graphs improve memory.”

**Total Recall:** Hindsight's ingest backbone was not controlled across benchmark cells, so cross-backbone/reasoning comparisons for Hindsight are invalid. The stronger conclusion is workload-dependent break-even and the failure of simple depth/message-size cost models for stateful memory pipelines.

**Agent Skills:** the result is not that skills are generally harmful. The useful claim is narrower: under matched executions, topically relevant reusable procedure can change required implementation/verification behavior or induce substantial extra work. Differential comparison is the important methodology.

**Sleeping Agent:** the one-sentence temporal-protection intervention is unusually diagnostic, but it is still one compression framework on LoCoMo; treat it as an early signal that compression needs field-level preservation contracts.
