## QC decisions

No paper received **5/5** in the seed run. High relevance was not treated as evidence of field-level importance.

`Activity Frames` was deliberately scored below the other accepted papers because the current empirical evidence is single-user/single-machine. `AuthMem-Bench` and `MAFIA` were retained even though they are evaluation/security papers because they reveal memory-specific invariants that affect future agent behavior: authority preservation and persistent-state integrity.

## Deferred, not rejected

Several relevant papers from the adjacent early-August window were discovered but left for the next backfill pass rather than rushed into the seed PR, including **V-Mem (2608.01543)**, **RoMeRL (2608.02508)**, and **PGMem (2608.01708)**. The scheduled curator should revisit them using the same full-text + adversarial-QC protocol.

## Boundary exclusions

Generic RAG, generic long-context modeling, and KV-cache optimization were excluded when persistent agent memory was not a central mechanism. A title or abstract merely mentioning “memory” was not sufficient for inclusion.

## Provenance rule

Paper URLs and analyzed full-text URLs are recorded in each canonical JSON record. Code/project links are included only when independently verified; unknown links remain `null` rather than guessed.
